## Privacy Policy {#privacy}

At the Network Data Exchange (NDEx), accessible from www.ndexbio.org, your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website and services. By using our platform, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our service.

- **Personal information**: when you register for an account or use specific features of our platform, we may collect information such as your name, email address, and any other details you provide during registration.
- **Usage data**: we automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited on the platform. This data helps us understand how our users interact with NDEx.
- **Cookies**: we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on the site. Cookies allow us to remember your preferences and login information for future visits.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

NDEx uses the information we collect in the following ways:

- To provide, operate, and maintain our websites and services.
- To improve and personalize your user experience.
- To communicate with you regarding updates, support, and other service-related information.
- To analyze how our website is used and to gather statistical data to enhance our platform.
- To prevent fraudulent activities and ensure security.

### 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your data in the following circumstances:

- **Service providers** - We may share your information with trusted third-party providers who help us operate our platform, such as analytics providers. For example, we use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. Google processes this data on our behalf to help us understand how users interact with NDEx. You can learn more about how Google uses your data by visiting [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en-US). Users can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- **Legal compliance** - We may disclose your information when required by law or in response to legal processes (e.g., subpoenas, court orders).

### 4. Data Security

While we take the security of your data very seriously and implement industry-standard practices to protect it, we cannot guarantee the privacy of data (1) transmitted over the web when using NDEx, (2) stored on NDEx, (3) collected by others whose software or sites are used by NDEx, (4) is processed by our software, or (5) in any other way that it has been made accessible to NDEx. Notably, while you can designate access to a network in NDEx as “private”, this only flags it for its treatment by our access control mechanisms; it does not in any way guarantee its privacy. For example, NDEx is not guaranteed to be secure storage for proprietary data or pre-publication academic data.

We take the following measures to protect your data:

- **Software updates** - Our platform is continuously monitored, and we keep all our software up to date with the latest security patches to ensure protection against potential vulnerabilities.
- **Password protection** - Account passwords stored in our system are encrypted using strong encryption algorithms, ensuring that they are not visible to anyone, including our team. We do not store passwords in plain text.

Despite our best efforts, no online system can guarantee absolute security. We recommend using strong, unique passwords and practicing good security habits to further protect your account and data.

### 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access** - You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction** - You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion** - You may request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
- **Withdrawal of consent** - If we process your data based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, please [Contact us](https://home.ndexbio.org/contact-us/).

### 6. Third-Party Links

NDEx may contain links to external websites that are not operated by us. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
